Dear Sir,

Some people teach you how to do the work. You taught us how to think about it: to stay curious, to keep building, and to stay calm on the days when nothing seems to work.

Thank you for your trust, your patience, and for always making time to guide us. Working with you at Zensia is more than a job. It is the best classroom we have ever had.

On your birthday, we wish you good health, long rides on open roads, memory cards full of beautiful frames, and a year in which everything you are building grows bigger than you imagined.

Happy Birthday, Sir. Keep leading, keep riding, keep smiling.
